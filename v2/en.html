Questions

What does Dibsly send to the AI?

Only what an answer needs, and only after you allow it on its own page. Without it everything else works. Dibsly works out the answer on your phone from your data; the AI only puts it into words.

Which banks does Dibsly support?

Free: monobank directly with a read-only token, statements from PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK, KredoBank and monobank, and Apple Pay from any card. With Premium your ChatGPT or Claude reads a statement from any bank.

Is it safe?

Dibsly never asks for a bank password and cannot move money. Without an account your data stays on your iPhone and in encrypted backups in your iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Details in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is for sync, the website, assistants and shared accounts.

Is there an Android version?

iPhone only for now. my.dibslyapp.com opens in any browser with Premium.
