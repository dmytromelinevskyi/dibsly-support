14 · Questions

Safe.No bank password.

Is it safe?

Yes. Dibsly never asks for your bank password and can’t move money, and without an account your data stays on your iPhone and in your own encrypted backups.

How much is it?

The basics are free. Premium is $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year.

Which banks?

monobank directly, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK, KredoBank and monobank statements (FOP too) and Apple Pay from any card, all free. Any other bank’s statement is read by the AI: 3 in all free, up to 25 a month with Premium.

What does the AI see?

Only what an answer needs, and only with your permission. Dibsly does the math on your phone; the AI just puts it into words.

Do I need to sign up?

No. An account is only for the website, sync, ChatGPT or Claude and shared accounts.

Android?

Not yet: iPhone, and my.dibslyapp.com in any browser (with Premium). iPad and Mac are next.
