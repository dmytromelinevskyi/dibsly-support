Questions

Which banks does Dibsly work with?

For free: Apple Pay from any card, statements of the Ukrainian banks monobank, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK and KredoBank as PDF, CSV or Excel, and monobank directly with a personal token. With Premium your ChatGPT or Claude reads a statement from any bank, and on iOS 27 Dibsly reads notifications from any bank.

How do ChatGPT and Claude work with Dibsly?

With Premium you connect your own assistant to your Dibsly account on the website. It sees your accounts and operations when you ask, and records or changes something only after you confirm. Every change goes into a journal where you can undo it. You can disconnect the assistant at any time.

Is it safe?

Dibsly never asks for your bank password. A monobank token only reads the statement and cannot move money. Without an account your data lives only on your iPhone and in encrypted backups in your iCloud Drive. There are no ads and no ad trackers. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. A Dibsly account is needed for sync, the website, assistants and shared accounts. Deleting the account erases all your data on the server.

Can I bring my data from another app?

Yes. Dibsly imports a Monefy CSV export, its own backups, bank statements and broker reports. Before saving, Dibsly shows exactly what it will add.

Is there an Android version?

For now Dibsly is iPhone only. The website my.dibslyapp.com opens in any browser; it needs an account and Premium.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Nothing disappears. Premium features switch off, receipts go back to 10 a month, and all your records stay where they are.
