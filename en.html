Coming from Monefy?

Questions

Which banks does Dibsly work with?

Apple Pay records from any card, and with Premium on iOS 27 Dibsly reads notifications from any bank. Statements of the Ukrainian banks monobank, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK and KredoBank are read from PDF, CSV or Excel, and monobank also connects directly with a personal token.

Is it safe?

Dibsly never asks for your bank password. A monobank token only reads the statement and cannot move money. Without an account your data lives only on your iPhone and in encrypted backups in your iCloud Drive. There are no ads and no ad trackers. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. A Dibsly account is only needed to see the same data on several iPhones and on the website, or to keep shared accounts. Deleting the account erases all your data on the server.

How do I move my data from Monefy?

Export your data from Monefy to CSV, then in Dibsly open Settings → Import & export → CSV file → Monefy CSV. Accounts, categories, currencies and transfers come along, and Dibsly shows what it will add before saving.

Is there an Android version?

For now Dibsly is iPhone only. The website my.dibslyapp.com opens in any browser; it needs an account and Premium.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

Nothing disappears. Premium features switch off, receipts go back to 10 a month, and all your records stay where they are.
