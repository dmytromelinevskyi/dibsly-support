About Dibsly

Dibsly is a personal finance tracker. Your records are kept on your device and in your own iCloud backups; the developer does not receive them. Dibsly is free to use; Dibsly Premium adds optional paid features.

Not financial advice

Dibsly shows and sorts your own records. Nothing in it is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Decisions about your money are yours.

AI can make mistakes

Dibsly's AI (OpenAI) reads receipt photos — 10 a month free, more with Premium — and, with Premium, sorts merchants, reads your bank notifications and the phrases your phone could not understand. It sends merchant names, your category names, receipt photos, those phrases and, if you set it up, the text of your bank notifications to OpenAI — only after you allow it. Its answers are guesses and may be wrong. Dibsly files only the ones it is fairly sure about and shows the rest as suggestions; you can change any category it chose or any receipt line it read. Check the result: you are responsible for your records.

Your data

Statements, monobank sync, Apple Pay, bank notifications and receipts can be incomplete or read wrongly. Check your balances against your bank and keep backups. Bank access is read-only: Dibsly can never move money. You can disconnect a bank or delete all data in Settings at any time.

Premium

Dibsly Premium adds bank notifications of any bank read by the AI (iOS 27 or later), AI categorization, up to 300 receipts a month and voice entry with the AI; everything else, including Investments, is free. Premium costs $1.99 a month or $19.99 a year (the App Store shows the price in your country) and is an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. The trial turns into a paid subscription, and each period renews, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions; refunds are handled by Apple. Apple’s Standard License Agreement also applies.
