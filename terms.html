About Dibsly

Dibsly is a personal finance tracker. Your records are kept on your device and in your own iCloud backups; the developer does not receive them. Dibsly is free to use; Dibsly Premium adds optional paid features.

Not financial advice

Dibsly shows and sorts your own records. Nothing in it is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Decisions about your money are yours.

AI can make mistakes

AI categorization (Premium) sends merchant names and your category names to OpenAI, and, if you set it up, the text of your bank notifications — only after you allow it. Its answers are guesses and may be wrong. Dibsly files only the ones it is fairly sure about and shows the rest as suggestions; you can change any category it chose. Check the result: you are responsible for your records.

Your data

Statements, monobank sync, Apple Pay and bank notifications can be incomplete or read wrongly. Check your balances against your bank and keep backups. Bank access is read-only: Dibsly can never move money. You can disconnect a bank or delete all data in Settings at any time.

Premium

Premium is a subscription bought through Apple. The free trial turns into a paid subscription unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings; refunds are handled by Apple. Apple’s Standard License Agreement also applies.

No warranty

Changes and contact
