# Dibsly landing

The landing introduces the real Dibsly iOS app through nine scroll-controlled stories: automatic purchase recording, quick entry, Ask Dibsly, planning, investments, debts, FOP, analytics and shared money. The user sees which native control is pressed, the transition and the resulting state. Reversing scroll reverses the narrative.

The visual reference is Lusion About: spatial background, large typography, continuous camera movement and scroll-driven storytelling. Dibsly keeps its warm orange identity and actual iOS interface. Apple/iOS informs readable hierarchy, restrained phone motion, realistic proportions and generous spacing.

Primary actions are App Store and the web app. Supporting pages, prices, support, policies and existing shared receipt links remain available. Ukrainian and English are equally complete.

Motion can be reduced. The manual screen sequence and interactive screen library keep all content accessible when animation is disabled or unavailable. Mobile text, phone and captions occupy separate reserved areas.
