First steps

  1. Install Dibsly from the App Store and go through the setup: language, main currency, first account.
  2. At Your banks, connect monobank or pick your bank's statement, and the history comes in at once.
  3. An account is optional. You can sign in with Apple, Google or e-mail any time later, from your profile on Main.
  4. Then let new spending record itself: Apple Pay and, with Premium, bank notifications.

Apple Pay through Shortcuts

Every Apple Pay payment becomes a draft in Dibsly: check the amount and category and save it with one tap. Free, with any card.

  1. In Dibsly open Settings → Transaction automation → Apple Pay and tap Install automation.
  2. In the Shortcuts app open the Automation tab, find Dibsly · Apple Pay and make sure it is on and runs immediately, without asking.
  3. The first payment with each card asks for its account in Dibsly; after that it is remembered.

By hand: a new Notification automation for the Wallet app, Run Immediately. Actions: Get text from Subtitle, Body and Title, a Text with the texts, then Dibsly's Import Wallet notification: Notification text — the text, Card — the card's name, Show When Run off.

Nothing arrives? Check that Wallet may show notifications (iOS Settings → Notifications → Wallet). With bank notifications on too, one purchase stays one draft.

Bank notifications Premium · iOS 27

Pushes from any bank — purchases, top-ups, transfers — are read by Dibsly with the AI and become drafts.

  1. In Dibsly open Settings → Transaction automation → Bank notifications and tap Install automation.
  2. In Shortcuts open the Dibsly · Bank Notifications automation and, under “When I receive a notification from”, pick your banks' apps: monobank, Privat24, UKRSIB online, KredoBank or any other.
  3. Make sure it runs immediately and that Show When Run is off in the Read notification action.
  4. Allow the AI in Dibsly when it asks: without it the notifications cannot be read.

Bank statements and monobank

  1. monobank directly: create a personal token at api.monobank.ua and paste it in Dibsly: Settings → Import & export → Connect monobank. The token only reads, and it stays on this iPhone.
  2. A statement: in your bank's app save a statement for the period you need — PDF, CSV, XLS or XLSX — and share it to Dibsly or open it in Settings → Import & export → Bank statement (or FOP). monobank, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK and KredoBank — and their FOP statements — Dibsly reads by itself on the phone, free. “How to export” shows where to get one.
  3. Check the preview — the account, the number of operations and the transfers between your accounts — and tap Add and continue. What Dibsly already has is not added twice.
  4. Any other bank: the statement is read by the AI in Dibsly — 3 statements in all for free, up to 25 a month with Premium. Check the amounts of such a statement before saving.

Brands and categories

  1. A brand in one tap: when recording an expense, open the category and switch to Brands — the logos of shops, cafés, carriers and services, laid out by your categories. A tap writes the brand's name and picks the category; hold a brand to pick another one.
  2. Start typing the name in the note — “silp”, “mcd”, “wolt” — and Dibsly offers the brand once per category you have used it with.
  3. Dibsly learns from your records: a brand goes where most of your operations with it went, and the note keeps your own way of writing it. One different category once does not change it.
  4. Move it for good: Settings → Brands, tap a brand and pick a category (or drag it onto one). It always goes there then — from statements and drafts too — and travels with backups, sync and the CSV export. “Back to automatic” undoes the move.

Investing

  1. Turn on Settings → Features → Investments if the Invest tab is not there yet.
  2. Save your broker's report: Interactive Brokers — the Activity Statement, Freedom Finance — the broker report as XLSX or PDF.
  3. Open Settings → Import & export → Broker report and pick the file. Dibsly sorts out buys, sales, dividends with the tax withheld and deposits.

Debts

Who owes whom and how much. Lent, borrowed or paid back is a transfer between your account and the person, so it is neither an expense nor an income. Dibsly only records it: you move the money yourself.

  1. Record a debt: on Main open Debts at the bottom, or tap + → Operation → Debt.
  2. Split an expense: in an expense's card, Split with… — your part stays an expense, your friends' parts become debts. For a receipt: Split the bill with “I paid the whole bill”.
  3. Paid back: on the person's page, Paid back or Pay back. Money from them in a bank notification is suggested as a return; a saved income becomes one with “It is a debt paid back” in its card.
  4. A link to the debt: until a friend accepts the debt, Share sends them its link. The friend accepts it in their own Dibsly, and every record either of you makes reaches the other to confirm. Then Remind sends them a push.
  5. A debt let go: Paid back → Money did not come to an account — the debt closes and the sum goes to Gifts in your expenses.

For the link both of you need a Dibsly account — a free one is enough. Without an account, Share sends only the reminder's text.

Credits

A loan, a mortgage, an instalment plan, a microloan or a credit card's limit: what is left, the next payment and the overpayment.

  1. Turn on Settings → Features → Credits: a Credits block appears at the bottom of Main.
  2. Open All › in that block and tap + to pick the kind of credit. A mortgage or an old loan is entered by what is left on a day.
  3. Bought in instalments: in a saved expense's card, Bought in instalments — the purchase stays whole in Analytics, the bank's part comes back to the card, and each month's part pays the instalment off.
  4. Paying: on a credit's page Pay records the payment and splits it into the body and interest (an expense in Interest and fees). Early shows what a shorter term or a lower payment saves. A payment from a statement: “This is a credit payment” in its card.
  5. Credit card and reminders: On hand shows only your own money, with “… of credit still available” under it. Payments show in the calendar with reminders three days before and on the day.

Dibsly only records the payments — it never moves money to the bank.

FOP (Ukrainian sole trader)

For a FOP of the 3rd group: income, the single tax, the military levy and ESV by quarter, deadlines and payment details. Free.

  1. Open Settings → Features → FOP and tap Turn on FOP. A short questionnaire: the group, the rate, ESV, your community, the FOP accounts.
  2. Income: money that arrives on a FOP account counts as income at the NBU rate of the day it arrived. A refund or your own transfer: turn off “FOP income” in the operation's card, or hold it on the FOP page → “Not income”.
  3. Taxes and deadlines: the FOP page shows the single tax, the military levy and ESV by quarter, how much is due and by when. The deadlines are in the calendar with reminders.
  4. Paying: Show payment details under the deadlines opens the amounts and the tax service's details for your community — every field copies with a tap.

Dibsly does not pay taxes or file the declaration: you pay and file them yourself. The figures and payment details are worked out from your records — check them in the taxpayer's e-cabinet, especially before the first payment.

Moving from another app

Dibsly imports a Monefy export and its own Dibsly CSV. In Monefy export your data to CSV, then in Dibsly open Settings → Import & export → CSV file → Monefy CSV. Accounts, categories, currencies and transfers come along, and Dibsly shows what it will add before saving.

Shared accounts Premium

  1. Sign in to a Dibsly account: your profile on Main → sign in with Apple, Google or e-mail.
  2. Open Profile → Sharing → Invite by link and send the link.
  3. The other person opens the link on an iPhone with Dibsly and taps Join. Then choose which account to share.
  4. One member's Premium is enough. You can leave any time, in Sharing too.

The website my.dibslyapp.com Premium

Open my.dibslyapp.com on your computer and sign in to the same Dibsly account as on your iPhone: with Apple, Google or an e-mail and a password. You get searchable history, analytics, the plan, the calendar and drafts; changes show up on your phone too.

Ask Dibsly

A chat about your money right in the app: ask in plain words, and Dibsly answers with your own figures.

  1. Where it is: on Main, the Dibsly button above “+”; on other pages, the orange tab on the right. The chat knows which page you opened it from.
  2. The first time Dibsly shows what goes to the AI and asks your permission — tap Allow. Turn it on or off in Settings → Features → AI in Dibsly.
  3. What it can do: it counts spending, finds operations, records (“record coffee 85”), moves them to categories, sets limits and imports a statement with 📎. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
  4. How many questions: 20 questions to the AI a month are free, up to 40 a day with Premium. Questions about the app itself (“where do I turn on FOP?”) Dibsly answers from the help, without the AI and not counted.

ChatGPT and Claude Premium

  1. On the website open Connect AI: it has the connection address https://my.dibslyapp.com/mcp and the steps for each assistant.
  2. Claude (claude.ai, the app): Settings → Connectors → add a custom connector with the address above, then sign in to Dibsly (Apple, Google or an e-mail and a password) and allow access.
  3. ChatGPT (chatgpt.com): Plugins → Add → Add custom MCP server, the address above, OAuth for authentication, then sign in to Dibsly the same way and allow access. On some ChatGPT plans it only reads and does not save — that is the plan's limit, not Dibsly's.
  4. Ask in plain words: “how much did I spend on cafés this month?”, “here is a statement, record it”. The assistant saves changes only after your “yes”, and each one can be undone in the AI journal on the website.
  5. Every connection is listed in the app: Profile → Devices and AI. You can disconnect them there too.

Backups and a new iPhone

  1. Settings → Backup & recovery: choose an iCloud Drive folder or connect Google Drive, make up a recovery password and turn on automatic backups.
  2. Keep the password in a password manager: a backup does not open without it, and Dibsly cannot reset it.
  3. On a new iPhone install Dibsly, choose Restore my data and the latest backup — or just sign in to your Dibsly account if you have sync (Premium).

Premium and cancelling

Premium adds sync and shared accounts, the website, ChatGPT and Claude, notifications from any bank and categories in one tap with the AI. The limits are higher: up to 300 receipts a month (10 without Premium), up to 25 statements of other banks a month (3 in all without Premium) and up to 40 chat questions a day (20 a month without Premium). Start it from your profile on Main or the Premium screen.

To cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dibsly, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Nothing is deleted when Premium ends.
