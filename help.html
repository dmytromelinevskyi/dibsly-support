First steps

  1. Install Dibsly from the App Store and go through the setup: language, main currency, first account.
  2. At Your banks, connect monobank or pick your bank's statement, and the history comes in at once.
  3. An account is optional. You can sign in with Apple any time later, from your profile on Main.
  4. Then let new spending record itself: Apple Pay and, with Premium, bank notifications.

Apple Pay through Shortcuts

Every Apple Pay payment becomes a draft in Dibsly: check the amount and category and save it with one tap. Free, with any card.

  1. In Dibsly open Settings → Transaction automation → Apple Pay and tap Install automation.
  2. In the Shortcuts app open the Automation tab, find Dibsly · Apple Pay and make sure it is on and runs immediately, without asking.
  3. The first payment with each card asks for its account in Dibsly; after that it is remembered.

By hand: a new Notification automation for the Wallet app, Run Immediately. Actions: Get text from Subtitle, Body and Title, a Text with the texts, then Dibsly's Import Wallet notification: Notification text — the text, Card — the card's name, Show When Run off.

Nothing arrives? Check that Wallet may show notifications (iOS Settings → Notifications → Wallet). With bank notifications on too, one purchase stays one draft.

Bank notifications Premium · iOS 27

Pushes from any bank — purchases, top-ups, transfers — are read by Dibsly with the AI and become drafts.

  1. In Dibsly open Settings → Transaction automation → Bank notifications and tap Install automation.
  2. In Shortcuts open the Dibsly · Bank Notifications automation and, under “When I receive a notification from”, pick your banks' apps: monobank, Privat24, UKRSIB online, KredoBank or any other.
  3. Make sure it runs immediately and that Show When Run is off in the Read notification action.
  4. Allow the AI in Dibsly when it asks: without it the notifications cannot be read.

Bank statements and monobank

  1. monobank directly: create a personal token at api.monobank.ua and paste it in Dibsly: Settings → Import & export → Connect monobank. The token only reads, and it stays on this iPhone.
  2. A statement: in your bank's app save a statement for the period you need — PDF (monobank also CSV or XLS) — and share it to Dibsly or open it in Settings → Import & export → Bank statement. monobank, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK and KredoBank are supported.
  3. Check the preview — the account, the number of operations and the transfers between your accounts — and tap Add and continue. What Dibsly already has is not added twice.
  4. Any other bank Premium: give the statement to your ChatGPT or Claude connected to Dibsly and ask it to record it. See ChatGPT and Claude.

Investing

  1. Turn the Invest tab on in Settings if it is not there yet.
  2. Save your broker's report: Interactive Brokers — the Activity Statement, Freedom Finance — the broker report as XLSX or PDF.
  3. Open Settings → Import & export → Broker report and pick the file. Dibsly sorts out buys, sales, dividends with the tax withheld and deposits.

Moving from another app

Dibsly imports a Monefy export and its own Dibsly CSV. In Monefy export your data to CSV, then in Dibsly open Settings → Import & export → CSV file → Monefy CSV. Accounts, categories, currencies and transfers come along, and Dibsly shows what it will add before saving.

Shared accounts Premium

  1. Sign in to a Dibsly account: your profile on Main → Sign in with Apple.
  2. Open Profile → Sharing → Invite by link and send the link.
  3. The other person opens the link on an iPhone with Dibsly and taps Join. Then choose which account to share.
  4. One member's Premium is enough. You can leave any time, in Sharing too.

The website my.dibslyapp.com Premium

Open my.dibslyapp.com on your computer and sign in to the same Dibsly account as on your iPhone: with Apple, Google or an e-mail and a password. You get searchable history, analytics, the plan, the calendar and drafts; changes show up on your phone too.

ChatGPT and Claude Premium

  1. On the website open Connect AI: it has the connection address https://my.dibslyapp.com/mcp and the steps for each assistant.
  2. Claude (claude.ai, the app): Settings → Connectors → add a custom connector with the address above, then sign in to Dibsly (Apple, Google or an e-mail and a password) and allow access.
  3. ChatGPT: Settings → Apps → Advanced → Developer mode, then add an app with the address above and sign in to Dibsly the same way. Availability depends on your OpenAI plan.
  4. Ask in plain words: “how much did I spend on cafés this month?”, “here is a statement, record it”. The assistant saves changes only after your “yes”, and each one can be undone in the AI journal on the website.

Backups and a new iPhone

  1. Settings → Backup & recovery: choose an iCloud Drive folder or connect Google Drive, make up a recovery password and turn on automatic backups.
  2. Keep the password in a password manager: a backup does not open without it, and Dibsly cannot reset it.
  3. On a new iPhone install Dibsly, choose Restore my data and the latest backup — or just sign in to your Dibsly account if you have sync (Premium).

Premium and cancelling

Premium adds sync and the website, ChatGPT and Claude, shared accounts, notifications from any bank, categories from the AI and up to 300 receipts a month. The first 7 days are free. Start it from your profile on Main or the Premium screen.

To cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dibsly, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Nothing is deleted when Premium ends.
