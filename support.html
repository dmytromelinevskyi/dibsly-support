Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. Dibsly never asks for your bank password and can’t move money, and without an account your data stays on your iPhone and in your own encrypted backups.

How much is it?

The basics are free. Premium is $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year.

Which banks?

monobank directly, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK, KredoBank and monobank statements (FOP too) and Apple Pay from any card, all free. Any other bank’s statement is read by the AI: 3 in all free, up to 25 a month with Premium.

What does the AI see?

Only what an answer needs, and only with your permission. Dibsly does the math on your phone; the AI just puts it into words.

Do I need to sign up?

No. An account is only for the website, sync, ChatGPT or Claude and shared accounts.

Android?

Not yet: iPhone, and my.dibslyapp.com in any browser (with Premium). iPad and Mac are next.

How do I cancel Premium?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dibsly, at least 24 hours before the paid period ends. Apple handles payments and refunds. When Premium ends nothing is deleted: your data stays; sync, the website and the assistants stop, and the limits go back to Free.

A new iPhone: how do I move my data?

Install Dibsly and choose “Restore my data” with your newest backup, or sign in to your Dibsly account (sync comes with Premium).

Backups

Where and when

An encrypted backup — by hand, or daily or weekly — to your iCloud Drive folder or to “Dibsly Backups” in Google Drive. Of the automatic ones, Dibsly keeps the five newest.

Recovery password

A backup opens only with your recovery password. Dibsly doesn’t know it and can’t reset it, so keep it in a password manager.

Imports

Banks

monobank, PrivatBank, UKRSIBBANK and KredoBank statements (and their FOP statements) are read by Dibsly itself, on your phone; the file is never uploaded. monobank can also be connected directly with a read-only token.

Any other bank’s statement is read by the AI: 3 in all free, up to 25 a month with Premium.

Apps and brokers

Bring a ledger over from a Dibsly or Monefy CSV, and investments from Interactive Brokers and Freedom Finance reports.

Your data

On your iPhone first

Without an account your records stay on the phone and in your own encrypted backups. With an account and Premium they sync through Dibsly’s server, so your iPhones and my.dibslyapp.com show the same.

The AI only with your permission

The AI is OpenAI, reached through Dibsly’s own server. Only what a feature needs is sent; OpenAI keeps it for at most 30 days and doesn’t train models on it.

Rates come from the National Bank of Ukraine, quotes from Nasdaq and Yahoo Finance; these requests carry none of your records. All the details are in the privacy policy.
