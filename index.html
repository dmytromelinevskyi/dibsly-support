Quick help

Backup & Recovery

Dibsly can create encrypted backups, manually or automatically, in an iCloud Drive folder you choose.

Recovery password

Your password is required to decrypt a backup. Dibsly does not receive it and cannot reset or recover it for you.

Import & Export

Dibsly reads statements from monobank, UKRSIBBANK, PrivatBank and KredoBank, connects to monobank with your own token, and imports its own CSV, Monefy CSV and supported Freedom Finance and Interactive Brokers reports.

Your data

Dibsly is local-first and does not require a Dibsly account. Financial data stays on your device unless you export or back it up, or allow AI categorization, which sends merchant names to OpenAI.

Dibsly Premium

AI categorization and Investments are a subscription with a free trial, bought through the App Store. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Apple handles payments and refunds.

When contacting support

  • Include your Dibsly version and build number from Settings.
  • Describe the steps that led to the problem.
  • Do not email your recovery password or unencrypted financial exports.
