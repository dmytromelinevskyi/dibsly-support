Quick help

Backup & Recovery

Dibsly can create encrypted backups in a folder you choose in iCloud Drive or in the visible Dibsly Backups folder on Google Drive.

Recovery password

Your password is required to decrypt a backup. Dibsly does not receive it and cannot reset or recover it for you.

Import & Export

Dibsly supports its own CSV format, Monefy CSV imports, and supported Freedom Finance and Interactive Brokers reports.

Your data

Dibsly is local-first and does not require a Dibsly account. Financial data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or back it up.

When contacting support

  • Include your Dibsly version and build number from Settings.
  • Describe the steps that led to the problem.
  • Do not email your recovery password or unencrypted financial exports.
